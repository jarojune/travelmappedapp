## Privacy policy

*Effective September 27, 2026*

This policy explains what Travel Mapped stores, where, and how you can
delete it. We don't show ads, don't use analytics or tracking, and don't sell
or share your data.

### What the app stores

- **Your account details.** You sign in with Google (or with an email
  address and password, for accounts we set up, such as one for app review).
  We store your name and profile picture link (Google accounts), your email
  address, an account ID, and when you last signed in, so the app can keep
  your trips under your account.
- **What you write.** Trip titles and dates; stop names, dates, notes and
  positions; journal entries; and photo captions.
- **Photos you add.** Photos are kept on your phone. If "Back up photos to
  the cloud" is on (it is by default, and you can turn it off in Settings),
  each photo is also uploaded as a full-size copy (or a high-quality
  compressed copy, if you choose), a screen-sized copy and a thumbnail. For
  each photo we also store its capture time, its size and dimensions, a
  fingerprint used to spot duplicates, and the location where it was taken
  if that is recorded in the photo (Android often removes it before the app
  sees the photo). We also keep a running total of the cloud space your
  photos use, to apply the backup allowance.
- **Your location, only when you ask.** If you add a stop "at my current
  location", the app reads your phone's location once, while you're using
  the app, and saves it as that stop's position. The app never tracks your
  location in the background.

### Where it is stored

Your account, what you write and your backed-up photos are stored with
Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore and Cloud Storage), which
processes the data on our behalf. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest.
Only you can read your data: the app's security rules limit every trip,
entry and photo to the account that created it.

### Other services the app uses

- **Map tiles** are loaded from [OpenFreeMap](https://openfreemap.org).
  Like any website, it receives your IP address and the map area being
  viewed. Tiles are cached on your phone.
- **Addresses for stops** are looked up with Android's built-in geocoder
  (provided by Google on most phones), which receives the stop's
  coordinates.
- **App Check** uses Google Play Integrity to confirm requests come from the
  genuine app, which protects your data from misuse.

### How long we keep it, and deleting it

Your data is kept until you delete it:

- Deleting a photo removes it from the cloud and your phone.
- Deleting a trip removes it, with its stops, journal entries and photos,
  permanently a few seconds later (after the chance to undo).
- **Deleting your account** permanently removes your account and everything
  in it. See [Delete your account](#delete-your-account).

Photos you saved to your phone's gallery with "Save to device" are ordinary
photos on your phone and are not affected. Google may keep deleted data in
its internal backups for a limited time before it is erased.

### Children

Travel Mapped is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly
collect data from them.

### Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update it here and change the date at the top.

## Delete your account

### In the app (immediate)

1. Open Travel Mapped and go to **Settings** (the gear on the trips screen).
2. Tap **Delete account**, read the warning, and tap **Delete everything**.
3. Confirm by signing in with the same Google account (or, for an email
   account, by entering your password).

Your account and all its data are deleted right away.

### Without the app

Email [juneapps@outlook.com](mailto:juneapps@outlook.com?subject=Delete%20my%20Travel%20Mapped%20account)
with the subject **"Delete my Travel Mapped account"**, sent from the email
address you use with Travel Mapped (so we can be sure
the request is yours). We'll delete the account within 30 days and reply to
confirm.

### What is deleted

- Your account and profile (name, email address, profile picture link).
- All your trips, stops, journal entries and captions.
- All your photos in the cloud, including their thumbnails and screen-sized
  copies, and their details (capture time and location).
- When deleted in the app: the photos the app keeps on that phone.

### What is kept

Nothing is kept by us. Photos you saved to your phone's gallery with
"Save to device" are ordinary photos on your phone and are not affected.
Google may keep deleted data in its internal backups for a limited time
before it is erased.
